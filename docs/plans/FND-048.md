# FND-048 – The narrow-screen tests measure the top bar after the data has arrived

**Phase:** 1 – Foundation
**Track:** B (see `TASKS.md`, "Two tracks")
**Depends on:** nothing open (found in the run of the pull request of GLB-001)
**Base branch:** `main`
**Read first:** `CLAUDE.md` §3 and §5, `docs/WORKFLOW.md`, `TASKS.md`
("Two tracks" and the entry of this task), `PROGRESS.md` (PLAN-036),

## Objective

No browser test of the narrow screen measures the top bar, or what lies
under it, before the data has arrived, so that none can fail by chance.

## Why

In the planner's run of the pull request of GLB-001 one browser test
failed once and passed the next time ("the data status and the date each
have a line", `Expected: 9`, `Received: -12`). A gate that can fail by
chance is a defect of the foundation: every later task goes through it,
and a failure that is nobody's teaches people to run again until it
passes. CLAUDE.md §3 does not let a known defect wait.

The cause: on a narrow screen the top bar is a line taller once the data
has arrived than while it loads (99 pixels and 78 at a width of 360),
because "Data updated …" and the day take a line each where "Loading
data…" and the day share one. Everything under the bar then lies a line
lower. A test that measures the bar before the data has arrived and
something else afterwards compares two layouts.

## What this task is, and what it is not

- **It is** a change to one file of browser tests,
  `frontend/e2e/narrow.spec.ts`: two small helpers, `open` and `again`,
  that load the page and wait for the data; every test of the file loads
  the page through them; and four new tests.
- **It is not** a change to the application. That the bar changes its
  height when the data arrives, so that the page jumps under the
  visitor's eyes, is a defect of its own, FND-049.
- **It is not** a longer time to pass, a retry or a skipped test. No
  expectation of an existing test changes.
- **It is not** a change to the shared fixture `e2e/fixtures.ts` or to
  any other file of browser tests: on a wide screen the bar has one line
  with or without the data, and the planner's runs with the data late
  found no other test that depends on when it comes.

## Design in short

- **`arrived(page)`** waits until the top bar says "Data updated". It is
  the wait `topBarPieces` had already; that helper now calls it.
- **`open(page)`** is `page.goto('./')` and then `arrived(page)`.
  **`again(page)`** is `page.reload()` and then `arrived(page)`.
- **Every test of the file loads the page through them.** The 19 places
  that said `await page.goto('./')` say `await open(page)`, and the two
  that said `await page.reload()` say `await again(page)`. Nothing else
  of those tests changes.
- **The rule is held by a test.** The file reads its own text and
  expects `page.goto(` once and `page.reload(` once: in the two helpers.
  A test that loads the page by itself fails that test, whatever the
  timing of the day.
- **The helpers are held by tests.** Three tests hold the data back for a
  second (a route on `data/manifest.json` that waits and then lets the
  request through) and check, with no waiting of their own, that after
  `open` the data status is there, that the bar then has the height it
  has with the data, and that after `again` the data status is there
  too.

Why all 19 and not only the tests that failed: see "What the planner
tried". Two tests fail when the data is simply late. Three more measure
the bar and then the globe stage or a panel, and fail when the data comes
between the two measurements; no fixed delay shows that, and it is the
same defect. The other tests of the file do not compare measurements
that the data changes, today. A rule with no exception costs nothing and
needs nobody to work out, for each new test, whether it is one of those.

## What the task left open, and what the trial settled

- **Which tests are at fault?** Five of the 19 the file had, at 14 of
  its 71 places (a test runs once for each width it is for). Two measure
  the bar first and wait for the data afterwards: "everything in the top
  bar is whole and inside it" (320, 360 and 390 pixels wide) and "the
  data status and the date each have a line" (360 and 390). Three
  measure the bar and then what lies under it, with no wait at all: "the
  top bar and the globe stage each have the whole width", "the sections
  panel lies over the globe stage, from the left" and "the details panel
  lies over the globe stage, from the right" (320, 360 and 390 each). At
  768 and 899 pixels the bar has room and does not change.
- **Does any other file of browser tests depend on when the data
  comes?** No. With the data late by 0.12, 0.3 and 1 second in every
  test of every file, all pass once this file is changed.
- **Does waiting make the suite slower?** Not so that it shows: the data
  arrives within a few hundredths of a second. The three tests that hold
  it back take a second or two each.
- **Is the wait in `topBarPieces` still needed?** Not by any test of
  today: every test has waited when it gets there. It stays, so that the
  helper is right by itself. A planted defect that takes it out is the
  one the tests do not find, and the plan says so below.
