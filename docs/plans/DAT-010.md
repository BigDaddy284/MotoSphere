## Objective

The store has a table of review items: what is in doubt about a record
and why, and whether a person has decided it, how, who and when.

## Why

"Do not automatically merge uncertain rider or track identities." and
"Use review queues when information conflicts." (CLAUDE.md §11). The
collecting will meet what it cannot settle by itself: two tracks that
may be one, a source that says another length than the record holds, a
value that the rules of its field refuse. It must not guess, and it
must not drop the doubt either. The first step is the place where a
doubt is kept until a person decides. It comes before the first table
of collected records (DAT-011), so that the first collector has
somewhere to put what it is not sure of.

## What this task is, and what it is not

The task as it stood named four things: the table, that the same doubt
is one item, the function that opens an item, and that a decided item
keeps its decision. The trial cut it down to the first, and the others
are tasks of their own (PLAN-029; CLAUDE.md §1: when uncertain, the
smaller task):

- **DAT-010, this task:** the table, its model with its two lists, its
  migration with the one index the table cannot be without, and its
  place on the list of the tables that hold no collected records.
- **DAT-050:** one doubt is one item: the store refuses a second item
  for the same doubt.
- **DAT-051:** one function opens an item, and gives back the item
  that is there when the doubt is one already.
- **DAT-052:** one function decides an item, and a decided item keeps
  its decision.

So after this task nothing opens an item yet, the same doubt can be
stored twice, and a decided item can be put back to open. Two tests
put the last two on record, and `DATABASE.md` says all three. That
the note is in no published file is held by the task that publishes
the detail files of the tracks (PUB-005), which already asks for it.

## Design in short

- **One table, `review_item`, one row for one doubt about one
  record.** Columns after the key: `kind`, `record_table`, `record_id`,
  `other_record_id`, `field`, `source_id`, `source_address`,
  `source_value`, `reason`, `state`, `decided_by`, `decided_at`, `note`,
  and the two times every table has.
- **Two lists**, in the module of the model. What is in doubt
  (`ReviewKind`): `possible duplicate`, `sources disagree`, `value
  outside its rules`. Whether it was decided (`ReviewState`): `open`,
  `accepted`, `rejected`. A new item is open.
- **The record is named as the history names it**, by the name of its
  table and its key there, for the same reason: an item can be about a
  record of any table.
- **What else an item names goes with its kind.** A possible duplicate
  names the other record (`other_record_id`, in the same table). The
  two other kinds name the field, the source that says what it should
  hold, the address at which the source says so, and what it says, as
  text.
- **Nine checks of its own:** the key of the record is above 0; the
  other record has the greater key; one check for each of the five
  columns that go with a kind; and two that hold who decided and when
  to the state. With the two of the lists, the five of the texts that
  are not empty and that of the web address, the table has seventeen.
- **One reference**, to the source, which says nothing of what happens
  when the source is deleted: SQLite then refuses.
- **One index**, `ix_review_item_source_id`, over the source.
- **The eighth migration**, `make_the_review_items`, in plain SQL,
  makes the table and the index. The schema is at version 8.
- **On the list** `NOT_COLLECTED`, with its reason, between
  `record_change` and `source`.

## What the task left open, and what the trial settled

- **"The records and sources involved": columns, or tables of their
  own?** Columns. A table of the records of an item and one of its
  sources would take any number of each, and nothing could then hold
  an item to what its kind needs, nor say when two items are the same
  doubt. Every doubt the design names is about one record and one
  other thing: another record that may be the same, or one source
  that says otherwise. Three records that may be one are three pairs;
  three sources that say three things are an item for each source
  that does not say what the record holds. So an item names its
  record, and then either the other record or a field with a source.
  The table can hold each kind to exactly that, with a check for each
  column.
- **One table for the three kinds, or one for each?** One. The record,
  the reason, the state, the decision and the note would be said three
  times, and "what waits for a decision" would be asked of three
  tables. The price is columns that are empty for some kinds. The five
  checks make sure that an item is wholly of its kind: the trial
  planted an item of each kind without each thing it needs, and with
  each thing it must not have, and every one is refused by the check
  of that column.
- **The pair of a possible duplicate is said in one way.**
  `other_record_id > record_id`: the record is the one with the
  smaller key. Without it "7 and 12" and "12 and 7" are two rows for
  one doubt, and DAT-050 could not tell them apart with an index. The
  same check refuses a record that is its own duplicate. Both records
  are of one table, so there is one `record_table`.
- **What the source says, and where.** The item keeps the value the
  source gives (`source_value`), because the record does not have it:
  it is exactly what was not stored. It is text, as the values of the
  history are, kept as it was given, empty text included. It is what a
  source said and nothing else: never run, never followed (CLAUDE.md
  §14). A test stores markup and a statement of SQL and reads each
  back letter for letter. The address at which the source says it
  (`source_address`) is kept with it, a web address like that of a
  collected record, so that a person can look ("Keep original source
  material or permitted evidence references so that records can be
  checked later.", DESIGN.md §8). When it was found is `created_at`.
- **Why, in words.** `reason` is text for the person who decides: that
  two tracks have one name and lie 140 metres apart, or what the
  record holds where the source says otherwise. The kind alone says
  what sort of doubt it is, and not why this one arose. Every item has
  one, of every kind.
- **The decision.** `state`, `decided_by` and `decided_at`, held
  together by two checks: an open item has neither who nor when, and
  an accepted or rejected item has both. The person is a name, as in
  the history, because the store has no table of people. `decided_at`
  is a column of its own and not `updated_at`: a note added later
  moves `updated_at`, and the moment of the decision must stay.
  Nothing holds `decided_at` to `created_at`; the function that
  decides (DAT-052) gives the moment.
- **What "accepted" and "rejected" mean** is said once, for every
  kind: what the item puts forward is taken, or it is not. For a
  possible duplicate that is "the two are one" or "the two are two";
  for the two other kinds "the field shall hold what the source says"
  or "the record stays as it is". What then happens to the records is
  for the tasks that merge and that apply decisions.
- **The note.** Text or none, on an item in any state. It never leaves
  the store; no file is published from this table yet, and PUB-005
  holds the published folder to it.
- **The index of the source comes with the table.** A test of DAT-045
  (`test_sqlite_looks_for_the_records_of_a_source_by_the_index_when_the_source_is_deleted`)
  reads SQLite's plan for deleting a source and holds that no table
  is read whole. A new table that refers to a source without an index
  fails it, which the trial saw by planting exactly that. So this
  index is not cut out as a task of its own, as the indexes of the
  runs and of the history were. The other indexes of the queue are.
- **The model gives a new item its state.** `default=ReviewState.OPEN`,
  as the runs get `running`. Given no state at all, the model stores
  an open item, so "an item without its state" is refused only past
  the model, and the tests say so.
- **Which check SQLite names.** It names the first that fails, in the
  order they stand in the table. A word that is not a kind is also
  "not a possible duplicate", so a row with such a word breaks the
  check of the other record before the check of the list unless the
  row is built for it. The tests of the two lists use a row that
  breaks nothing else. The trial got this wrong at first and the test
  said so.
- **The table holds no collected records**, so it goes on the list
  with its reason (DAT-046). Taken off the list, the rule names it
  three times: without six of the eight columns, and with `source_id`
  and `source_address` not as the set has them, because both can be
  without a value here. The test that takes each real table off the
  list in turn gets that case.

### 1. `backend/src/motosphere/db/models/review_item.py` – new, the whole file

```python
"""The review queue: what waits for a person's decision.

Accuracy is more important than quantity (DESIGN.md 8, CLAUDE.md 11).
Where the collecting cannot tell by itself what is right, it does not
guess. Two records that may be the same thing are not merged, a record
is not written over because another source says otherwise, and a value
that the rules of its field refuse is not stored. What is in doubt is
put down as an item of the review queue, and it waits there until a
person decides. This module has the model of the table that keeps the
items, :class:`ReviewItem`, and the two lists an item is said by:
:class:`ReviewKind`, what is in doubt, and :class:`ReviewState`, whether
it was decided and how.

An item is about one record. Like a change in the history, it names the
record by the name of its table and its key there, because it can be
about a record of any table, and SQLite does not hold the two to a row
that exists: a reference is to one table.

What else an item names goes with its kind. A possible duplicate names
the other record, which is of the same table. The two other kinds name
a field of the record, the source that says what the field should hold,
the address at which it says so, and what it says.
"""

from datetime import datetime
from enum import StrEnum

from sqlalchemy import CheckConstraint, ForeignKey, Index
from sqlalchemy.orm import Mapped, mapped_column

from motosphere.db.base import Base
from motosphere.db.lists import OneOf
from motosphere.db.texts import NotEmpty
from motosphere.db.web import WebAddress

__all__ = ["ReviewItem", "ReviewKind", "ReviewState"]


class ReviewKind(StrEnum):
    """What is in doubt."""

    # Two records of one table may be the same thing.
    POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE = "possible duplicate"
    # A source says that a field of a record holds another value than
    # the record holds.
    SOURCES_DISAGREE = "sources disagree"
    # A source says that a field of a record holds a value which the
    # rules of the field refuse.
    VALUE_OUTSIDE_ITS_RULES = "value outside its rules"


class ReviewState(StrEnum):
    """Whether an item was decided, and how."""

    # Nobody has decided it yet.
    OPEN = "open"
    # What the item puts forward is taken: the two records are one, or
    # the field shall hold what the source says.
    ACCEPTED = "accepted"
    # It is not taken: the two records are two, or the record stays as
    # it is.
    REJECTED = "rejected"


class ReviewItem(Base):
    """One doubt: the record it is about, what is in doubt and why, and what was decided.

    The table itself refuses an item that does not name what goes with
    its kind, or names what does not; a record that is said to be its own
    duplicate; a decision without who made it and when; and who or when
    without a decision.
    """

    __tablename__ = "review_item"
    __table_args__ = (
        CheckConstraint("record_id > 0", name="record_id_positive"),
        # The two records of a possible duplicate are said in one way:
        # the smaller key is the record, the greater the other. So no
        # record is its own duplicate, and a pair is not two pairs.
        CheckConstraint("other_record_id > record_id", name="other_record_id_after_record_id"),
        # What goes with the kind. A possible duplicate names the other
        # record and nothing else; the two other kinds name the field,
        # the source, where it says so and what it says, and no other
        # record.
        CheckConstraint(
            "(other_record_id IS NULL) = (kind != 'possible duplicate')",
            name="other_record_id_when_possible_duplicate",
        ),
        CheckConstraint(
            "(field IS NULL) = (kind = 'possible duplicate')",
            name="field_unless_possible_duplicate",
        ),
        CheckConstraint(
            "(source_id IS NULL) = (kind = 'possible duplicate')",
            name="source_id_unless_possible_duplicate",
        ),
        CheckConstraint(
            "(source_address IS NULL) = (kind = 'possible duplicate')",
            name="source_address_unless_possible_duplicate",
        ),
        CheckConstraint(
            "(source_value IS NULL) = (kind = 'possible duplicate')",
            name="source_value_unless_possible_duplicate",
        ),
        # Who decided and when are known of an item that was decided,
        # and of no other.
        CheckConstraint("(decided_by IS NULL) = (state = 'open')", name="decided_by_when_decided"),
        CheckConstraint("(decided_at IS NULL) = (state = 'open')", name="decided_at_when_decided"),
        # SQLite looks for the items that name a source before it lets
        # the source go, and indexes nothing by itself for a reference.
        Index(None, "source_id"),
    )

    kind: Mapped[ReviewKind] = mapped_column(OneOf(ReviewKind))
    # The record the item is about: the name of its table, and its key there.
    record_table: Mapped[NotEmpty]
    record_id: Mapped[int]
    # The other record of a possible duplicate, by its key in the same table.
    other_record_id: Mapped[int | None]
    # The field that is in doubt, by the name of its column.
    field: Mapped[NotEmpty | None]
    # The source that says what the field should hold, the address at
    # which it says so, and what it says, as text. That text is what a
    # source said and no more: it is kept as it was given, and it is
    # never run or followed.
    source_id: Mapped[int | None] = mapped_column(ForeignKey("source.id"))
    source_address: Mapped[WebAddress | None]
    source_value: Mapped[str | None]
    # Why the item is in doubt, in plain words, for the person who decides.
    reason: Mapped[NotEmpty]
    state: Mapped[ReviewState] = mapped_column(OneOf(ReviewState), default=ReviewState.OPEN)
    # The person who decided, by name, and when.
    decided_by: Mapped[NotEmpty | None]
    decided_at: Mapped[datetime | None]
    # What a person wants kept with the item. It never leaves the store.
    note: Mapped[NotEmpty | None]
```

### 2. `backend/tests/integration/test_review_item.py` – new, the whole file, 89 tests

```python
"""Tests of the table of the review queue, against a real SQLite file.

The store of every test is built by the migrations, as a real store is.
What the table must refuse is also written past the model.
"""

from collections.abc import Iterator
from contextlib import contextmanager
from datetime import UTC, datetime

import pytest
from sqlalchemy import Engine, delete, select
from sqlalchemy.exc import IntegrityError

from motosphere.db.models import ReviewItem, Source
from motosphere.db.models.review_item import ReviewKind, ReviewState
from motosphere.db.models.source import CollectionMethod, SourceCategory
from motosphere.db.work import unit_of_work

MOMENT = "2026-11-26T09:00:00.000000+00:00"
DECIDED = datetime(2026, 11, 26, 12, 30, tzinfo=UTC)
DECIDED_AS_TEXT = "2026-11-26T12:30:00.000000+00:00"
ADDRESS = "https://federation.example/tracks/7"
BROKEN_KEY = "FOREIGN KEY constraint failed"
CHECK = "CHECK constraint failed: ck_review_item_"
# A row written past the model: every column but the key, in the table's order.
PAST_THE_MODEL = (
    "INSERT INTO review_item (kind, record_table, record_id, other_record_id, field, "
    "source_id, source_address, source_value, reason, state, decided_by, decided_at, note, "
    "created_at, updated_at) VALUES (?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?)"
)
WHAT_IT_HOLDS = (
    "SELECT kind, record_table, record_id, other_record_id, field, source_id, source_address, "
    "source_value, reason, state, decided_by, decided_at, note FROM review_item ORDER BY id"
)
# The two kinds that are about what a source says of a field.
OF_A_FIELD = ["sources disagree", "value outside its rules"]
# What goes with those two kinds, and with them alone.
WITH_A_FIELD = ["field", "source_id", "source_address", "source_value"]


@contextmanager
def refused(why: str) -> Iterator[None]:
    """SQLite refuses what the block writes, and says exactly this."""
    with pytest.raises(IntegrityError) as refusal:
        yield
    assert str(refusal.value.orig) == why


def a_source(store: Engine, name: str = "Example Federation") -> int:
    """Store a source, and give its key."""
    source = Source(
        name=name,
        website="https://federation.example",
        category=SourceCategory.GOVERNING_BODY,
        collection_method=CollectionMethod.RSS,
    )
    with unit_of_work(store) as work:
        work.add(source)
    return source.id


def a_duplicate(**given: object) -> dict[str, object]:
    """A possible duplicate that nobody has decided, as a row, but for what is given."""
    row: dict[str, object] = {
        "kind": "possible duplicate",
        "record_table": "track",
        "record_id": 7,
        "other_record_id": 12,
        "field": None,
        "source_id": None,
        "source_address": None,
        "source_value": None,
        "reason": "The two tracks have one name and lie 140 metres apart.",
        "state": "open",
        "decided_by": None,
        "decided_at": None,
        "note": None,
    }
    assert set(given) <= set(row)
    row.update(given)
    return row


def of_a_field(kind: str = "sources disagree", **given: object) -> dict[str, object]:
    """An item about what the first source says of a field, as a row, but for what is given."""
    return a_duplicate(
        **{
            "kind": kind,
            "other_record_id": None,
            "field": "length_in_metres",
            "source_id": 1,
            "source_address": ADDRESS,
            "source_value": "1750",
            "reason": "The record says 1600.",
            **given,
        }
    )


def write(store: Engine, row: dict[str, object]) -> None:
```
